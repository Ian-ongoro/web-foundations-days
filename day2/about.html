About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed for fast, frictionless note-taking directly inside your browser. It helps busy individuals capture ideas, build lists, and organize daily tasks instantly without complex setups or slow loading times.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click anywhere inside the text area to start typing your note.
  2. Use custom keyboard shortcuts to instantly format text or save your changes.
  3. Review, edit, or clear your logs directly from your workspace dashboard.

Features

  • Autosave

    Real-time local storage autosave functionality keeps your data safe.

  • Markdown

    Clean, distraction-free markdown interface for fast writing.

  • Offline Ready

    Offline access capable for drafting notes anywhere, anytime.
